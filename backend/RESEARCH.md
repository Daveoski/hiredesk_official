Questions:

1. How do you currently receive applications (email, WhatsApp, spreadsheet, other)?
2. How do you keep track of which stage each candidate is in?
3. Has a candidate ever been forgotten or answered late? What happened?
4. Who takes part in interviews, and how do they share their opinion afterwards?
5. How do you schedule interviews, and have two interviews ever clashed?
6. Who makes the final hiring decision, and what information do they use?
7. What would make you trust a tool like this with candidate CVs?
8. How many jobs are open at the same time on average?
